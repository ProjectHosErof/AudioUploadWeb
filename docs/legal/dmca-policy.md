# Copyright Policy (DMCA)

**Effective date:** [EFFECTIVE DATE]

Project Hos Erof respects copyright. Recordings are contributed by members of the community. Most Coptic hymns are centuries-old traditional works, but a particular **recording** or **arrangement** can still belong to someone. If you believe a recording on our Service infringes your copyright, tell us as described below.

## 1. Sending a notice
Send a written notice to our designated copyright agent:

> **Copyright Agent, Project Hos Erof**
> [OPERATOR LEGAL NAME]
> [MAILING ADDRESS]
> Email: **copyright@hoserof.org**

Under 17 U.S.C. §512(c)(3), your notice must include:
1. your physical or electronic signature;
2. the copyrighted work you believe is infringed;
3. the material you believe infringes it, with enough detail for us to find it (for example, the hymn, season and service, and your best description of the recording);
4. your name, address, telephone number and email address;
5. a statement that you believe in good faith the use isn't authorized by the copyright owner, its agent or the law;
6. a statement that the information in your notice is accurate and, **under penalty of perjury**, that you're the copyright owner or authorized to act for them.

Knowingly misrepresenting that material is infringing can make you liable for damages (17 U.S.C. §512(f)).

## 2. What we do
When we receive a valid notice, we promptly remove or disable access to the recording. We tell the contributor if they gave us an email address, and we include a copy of the notice.

## 3. Counter-notice
If your recording was removed and you believe that was a mistake or misidentification, send our agent a counter-notice containing:
1. your physical or electronic signature;
2. the recording that was removed;
3. a statement, **under penalty of perjury**, that you believe in good faith it was removed by mistake or misidentification;
4. your name, address and telephone number, and a statement that you consent to the jurisdiction of the federal district court for your address (or, if you're outside the United States, any judicial district where we may be found), and that you'll accept service of process from the person who sent the original notice.

We'll forward it to the person who sent the notice. Unless they tell us within 10 business days that they've filed a court action, we'll restore the recording 10 to 14 business days after receiving your counter-notice.

## 4. Repeat infringers
We end, in appropriate circumstances, the accounts of contributors who repeatedly infringe, and we stop accepting their recordings.

## 5. Other concerns
For concerns that aren't about copyright (for example, a recording of you that you didn't agree to share), write to **hello@hoserof.org**. We'll look into it and, where appropriate, remove it.
