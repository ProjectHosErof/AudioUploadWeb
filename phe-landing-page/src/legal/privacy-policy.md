# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** [EFFECTIVE DATE]

Project Hos Erof ("**we**", "**us**") collects recordings of Coptic hymns so we can preserve them and build tools that recognize them. This policy explains what we collect on hoserof.org, why, who helps us run the service, and the choices you have. It's written to match how the site actually works. If that ever changes, we'll update this page first.

## 1. What we collect

### 1.1 When you upload a recording
- **The audio file**, exactly as you upload it. Audio files can contain information your phone or recording app added, such as the device model, the app, or sometimes a **location**. We don't read or use that information, and we remove it before any recording is shared outside the project (see section 4). If you'd rather it never reach us, remove it before uploading.
- **The file's original name**, its size and its format.
- **What you tell us about it**: the season, the service, the hymn and the language(s).
- **Your email address, only if you give it**, plus whether you'd like updates. It lets us tell you when your recording has been reviewed, and, if you ticked the box, send you project news.
- **The version of our Terms you agreed to** when you uploaded.

We also calculate technical facts about each recording, such as its length, its audio format and a fingerprint used to spot duplicates.

### 1.2 If you create an account
You can sign in with Google or with a link sent to your email. We receive your **email address**, your **name** as Google provides it (if you use Google), and an account identifier. We use these to show you your contributions, and to connect recordings you submitted earlier with the same email address once you've confirmed you own it.

### 1.3 If you join the mailing list
We keep your **email address**, when you **confirmed** it, where you signed up (the "Stay Informed" form or the upload form), and, if you leave, **when you unsubscribed**. We keep that last record after you leave so we never email you again.

### 1.4 Automatically
- **Your IP address** is used, at the moment you make a request, to limit abuse (rate limiting) and to check you're not a bot. For that check we use Cloudflare Turnstile, which looks at signals from your browser and device. We don't store your IP address in our database. Our hosting provider's short-lived operational logs may record it.
- **Your sign-in session** is kept in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in.
- We use **no advertising, no analytics trackers and no tracking cookies**.

## 2. How we use it
- To **store, review and catalogue** recordings, and to use them to **build and improve a hymn-recognition model** and related tools for Project Hos Erof (see the Contributor License in our [Terms](/terms)).
- To **show you your contributions** and their status.
- To **email you** a daily summary of review decisions on recordings you submitted with your email address or while signed in, and, only if you opted in and confirmed, project updates. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe.
- To **protect the service** from spam, abuse and attacks.
- To publish **aggregate statistics**, such as "N hours collected". These never identify you.

We **do not sell** your personal information, use it for advertising, or use recordings to identify who is singing.

## 4. Recordings outside the project
Recordings are used **within Project Hos Erof**. We don't publish or sell the collection as a dataset. Contributor names and email addresses are never included in the training data; that data refers to contributors only by an internal ID. If we ever want to share recordings more widely, we'll ask for new permission rather than relying on this policy. We'll remove embedded file information (section 1.1) before any recording leaves the project.

## 5. How long we keep it
- **Recordings and their details:** for as long as the project runs, unless you withdraw them (section 6).
- **Unfinished uploads:** deleted automatically within about 30 minutes.
- **Your email for review summaries:** for as long as the recording it came with is kept.
- **Mailing list:** until you unsubscribe. After that we keep only a do-not-email record.
- **Backups:** we keep encrypted backups so the collection can't be lost. When something is deleted, it leaves our backups **within 45 days**.

## 6. Your choices and rights
Wherever you live, you can ask us to:
- **see** the personal information we hold about you,
- **correct** it,
- **delete** it, including **withdrawing your recordings**,
- **stop emails**: use the unsubscribe link, or write to us.

Email **hello@hoserof.org** from the address you used, and we'll respond within 30 days. When you withdraw a recording, we remove it from the collection and stop using it for future training. A model already trained with it can't "unlearn" one recording, but no copy of your recording is kept inside the model.

## 7. Children
Contributors must be **13 or older**. A parent or guardian may submit recordings in which their child sings, as can a church servant who has the parent's permission. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have, we'll delete it. Parents can contact us at hello@hoserof.org.

## 8. Security
We protect information with encrypted connections, access controls, database row-level security, limited-access keys and encrypted backups kept in a separate account. No service is perfectly secure. If a breach affects you, we'll tell you as the law requires.

## 9. International visitors
We're based in the United States, and our providers store data in the United States and Canada. By using the site from elsewhere, you understand your information will be handled there.

## 10. Changes
If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change materially affects how we use information we already have, we'll tell contributors and subscribers by email before it takes effect.
